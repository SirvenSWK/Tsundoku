Introducing Tsundoku

Got a pile of things to do and no idea where to start?

Tsundoku turns messy thoughts like:

"I have a physics test Thursday, a history presentation Monday, need to call a friend sometime this week, and I should probably clean my room..."

into an organized, realistic plan.

How it works

1. Dump it.
Write your tasks naturally. No formal language needed.
2. Organize it.
Tsundoku understands what you mean, detects deadlines, and can break complicated goals into smaller steps.

3. Find the time.
Tsundoku looks at your existing schedule and suggests suitable times to get things done.

4. Put it on your calendar.
Once you approve a plan, Tsundoku can add it to your Google Calendar.

Curious about the name?

Tsundoku is a Japanese word describing the habit of buying books and letting them pile up unread.

Tasks can pile up too.

Tsundoku is here to help turn that pile into a plan.
